MoonPlayer vs Alternatives — Best IPTV Player for Norway 2026

MoonPlayer is a lightweight IPTV player for Android. While it works as a basic M3U player, Norwegian users who want a full-featured experience — electronic programme guide (EPG) for NRK and TV2, catch-up TV, multi-account support, and a modern interface — typically switch to one of these alternatives after trying MoonPlayer:

  • TiviMate — the gold standard for Android and Firestick. Best EPG, catch-up TV, and playlist management. Compatible with NorwayIPTV Xtream Codes.
  • IPTV Smarters Pro — the most universally compatible player. Works on Android, iOS, Smart TV and PC. Free download, native Xtream Codes support.
  • GSE Smart IPTV — best for Apple TV, iPhone and iPad users in Norway.

MoonPlayer IPTV — Setup with NorwayIPTV

If you still want to use MoonPlayer with NorwayIPTV, you need your M3U URL (included in your welcome email). Open MoonPlayer, tap "Add playlist", select "Remote URL", and paste the M3U link. Your full NorwayIPTV channel list — including NRK, TV2, TV2 Sport and 50,000+ other channels — will load automatically.

Note: MoonPlayer does not support Xtream Codes API natively, which means no catch-up TV and a slower EPG update. For the best NorwayIPTV experience, IPTV Smarters Pro or TiviMate is strongly recommended.
