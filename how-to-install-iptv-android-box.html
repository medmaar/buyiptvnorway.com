Android Box IPTV Setup — 6 Steps

1

Connect your Android Box

Plug your Android TV box (Formuler, NVIDIA Shield, Xiaomi Mi Box, Beelink, etc.) into your TV via HDMI and connect to Wi-Fi or Ethernet.

2

Open Google Play Store

From the Android TV home screen, open Google Play Store and search 'IPTV Smarters Pro' or 'TiviMate'.

3

Install IPTV Smarters Pro or TiviMate

Install your preferred player. IPTV Smarters Pro is free. TiviMate is also free (premium features via TiviMate Companion app).

4

Open IPTV Smarters Pro → Add User

Tap Add User → Xtream Codes API. Enter your NorwayIPTV portal URL, username and password from your welcome email.

5

Load channels

Tap 'Add User' and wait 30–60 seconds. All 50,000+ channels, EPG and VOD load automatically.

6

Set up TiviMate (alternative)

Open TiviMate → + → Add Playlist → Xtream Codes. Enter the same NorwayIPTV credentials. TiviMate offers a cleaner EPG and recording features.

💡 Pro Tip

Use an Ethernet cable instead of Wi-Fi for your Android box — a wired connection eliminates 90% of buffering issues on even the cheapest box.

Android Box IPTV — Full Guide 2026

An Android TV box is the most versatile IPTV platform — it runs Android TV OS, has access to Google Play Store, and supports every major IPTV player app. Unlike Smart TVs which are tied to one OS and limited app stores, an Android box plugs into any TV and gives you full app freedom.

IPTV Smarters Pro vs TiviMate on Android Box

IPTV Smarters Pro is the most popular IPTV player on Android — free, supports Xtream Codes and M3U, full EPG, VOD. TiviMate is preferred by experienced IPTV users — cleaner EPG interface, recording, multi-screen PiP, favourite channels. Both work perfectly with NorwayIPTV. Use Smarters Pro if you're new; switch to TiviMate once you're comfortable.

Kodi on Android Box for IPTV

Kodi is a powerful media centre app available on Android TV boxes. Install from Google Play, then add the PVR IPTV Simple Client add-on (Add-ons → My Add-ons → PVR Clients). Enter your NorwayIPTV M3U URL as the source. All channels, EPG and recordings are handled within Kodi's interface.

Best Android Box for 4K IPTV

For 4K IPTV, choose a box with: Android TV (not basic Android), 4 GB RAM minimum, dedicated 4K/HDR decoder chip, and Gigabit Ethernet port. The NVIDIA Shield TV Pro meets all these criteria. For a budget 4K IPTV box, the Xiaomi Mi Box S (4K edition) is the most popular choice at €40.

Android Box IPTV — FAQ

What is the best Android box for IPTV in 2026? +
NVIDIA Shield TV Pro is the premium pick — most powerful, best for 4K. Formuler Z11 Pro Max has a built-in portal. Xiaomi Mi Box S and Beelink GT-King are great budget options. All work with NorwayIPTV.
Does IPTV Smarters Pro work on all Android TV boxes? +
Yes — IPTV Smarters Pro works on any Android TV box running Android 5.0+. Available on Google Play Store or as an APK sideload.
What is the difference between Android TV and Android? +
Android TV is optimized for the big screen with remote navigation. Regular Android runs on phones/tablets. Android TV boxes (Shield, Formuler, Mi Box) run Android TV and are best for IPTV.
Can I use Kodi with NorwayIPTV on Android Box? +
Yes — install Kodi from Google Play, add the PVR IPTV Simple Client add-on, and enter your NorwayIPTV M3U URL. Full live TV, EPG and VOD inside Kodi.
How much RAM does an Android box need for IPTV? +
Minimum 2 GB RAM for HD IPTV. 4 GB RAM recommended for 4K and multitasking. The NVIDIA Shield TV Pro (16 GB RAM) handles the most demanding 4K streams flawlessly.
How do I fix buffering on my Android IPTV box? +
Check your internet speed (25 Mbps+ for 4K). If Wi-Fi signal is weak, use an Ethernet cable. Close background apps. If NorwayIPTV streams specifically buffer, contact WhatsApp support — response in 5 minutes.
