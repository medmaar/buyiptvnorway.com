How MAG Boxes Work

MAG boxes by Infomir are Linux-based IPTV set-top boxes (the MAG 410 was an Android exception). Instead of installing apps, a MAG box connects to a portal URL, and your IPTV provider links your subscription to the box's MAC address. Once set, it boots straight into live TV — ideal for less technical users.

Reading the model numbers

  • 25x (254, 256): older Full HD boxes, H.264 only.
  • 32x (322, 324): Full HD with HEVC (H.265) support.
  • 41x / 42x (410, 420, 424, 425A): first 4K generation.
  • 52x (520, 522, 524): current 4K HEVC generation. Per Infomir's datasheet, the MAG 524 runs Linux 4.9 with Dolby Digital Plus support.
  • w1 / w3 suffix: built-in Wi-Fi (w3 = newer dual-band 802.11ac module).

Set Up NorwayIPTV on a MAG Box

  1. 1
    Send us your MAC — The MAC address is printed on the sticker under the box (starts with 00:1A:79). Send it to support when ordering or requesting a trial.
  2. 2
    Open portal settings — Settings → System settings → Servers → Portals.
  3. 3
    Enter the portal URL — Type the portal URL from your welcome message into Portal 1 and give it a name.
  4. 4
    Reboot — Restart the box. It loads the portal and your channel list.
  5. 5
    Use Ethernet — MAG boxes are designed for wired networks; models without a w-suffix have no Wi-Fi at all.

MAG Boxes — What Buyers Ask

MAG 254 in 2026: still usable?

The MAG 254 was hugely popular, but it only decodes H.264. Channels delivered in HEVC (H.265) won't play, and the menus are slow with large channel lists. If you're buying today, a MAG 520 or 524 costs a little more and avoids these limits.

MAG 322 vs MAG 524

The 322 is a Full HD box with HEVC — a sensible budget choice for an HD TV. The 524 adds 4K, newer audio support and a faster chipset. For a 4K TV, choose the 52x series.

Can I watch on a MAG box over Wi-Fi?

Only “w” models have built-in Wi-Fi. For everyone else, use Ethernet or a powerline adapter. Even on w3 models, a cable gives the most stable picture during live sport.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Which MAG box is best for IPTV? +

The MAG 524 (or 524w3 with Wi-Fi) is Infomir's current 4K HEVC model and the best pick. The MAG 520 is a cheaper 4K alternative.

Is the MAG 254 still good? +

It's a legacy Full HD box without HEVC support. It still plays H.264 channels but struggles with large lists and can't play HEVC streams.

What do w1 and w3 mean on MAG boxes? +

They indicate built-in Wi-Fi. w3 is the newer dual-band 802.11ac module; boxes without a w suffix have no Wi-Fi.

How do I add a portal on a MAG box? +

Go to Settings → System settings → Servers → Portals, enter the portal URL from NorwayIPTV, save and reboot. Your MAC address must be registered with us first.

Where is the MAC address on a MAG box? +

On the sticker under the box, and in Settings → Device info. It usually begins with 00:1A:79.
