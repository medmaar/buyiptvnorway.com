What is IPTV and how does it work in Norway?

IPTV (Internet Protocol Television) delivers live TV channels and on-demand video over your internet connection instead of a satellite dish or cable. In Norway, you connect NorwayIPTV to an app like IPTV Smarters Pro or TiviMate, enter your credentials, and instantly get 50,000+ channels including NRK, TV2, Viaplay, and international sports — on any device.
