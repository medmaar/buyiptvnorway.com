Samsung vs LG for IPTV — Which Should You Buy?

⭐ 4.8/5 from 847 verified reviews ✅ 10,000+ active subscribers 🕐 24/7 WhatsApp support 🔁 No contract — cancel anytime

Both Samsung and LG Smart TVs work excellently with NorwayIPTV. The IPTV setup process is nearly identical on both: install Smart IPTV, find the MAC address, load M3U URL via siptv.app. The difference is in IPTV app variety and display panel technology.

Samsung Tizen IPTV — More App Options

Samsung Smart Hub has more IPTV apps: Smart IPTV (recommended), SET IPTV (free), Flex IPTV (modern UI) and Nanomid. SET IPTV being free and available on Samsung is a significant advantage for users who don't want to pay the Smart IPTV licence fee (~€5.49).

LG webOS IPTV — Best Picture Quality

LG's OLED panels deliver better picture quality than Samsung QLED for IPTV — deeper blacks, better contrast, no backlight bloom on dark scenes. Smart IPTV works on LG webOS via the LG Content Store, with the same siptv.app web panel setup. NET IPTV is LG's most popular IPTV app alternative.

Verdict

For IPTV specifically: Samsung wins on app variety (SET IPTV free). For overall picture quality: LG OLED wins. Both are excellent — your TV purchase decision should be based on panel type (QLED vs OLED) and budget, not IPTV app availability, since both support NorwayIPTV equally well.

Samsung vs LG IPTV — FAQ

Which Smart TV is better for IPTV — Samsung or LG? +
Both work well with NorwayIPTV. Samsung Tizen has Smart IPTV, SET IPTV and Flex IPTV. LG webOS has Smart IPTV, NET IPTV and Nanomid. The apps and setup process are nearly identical.
Does Smart IPTV work on both Samsung and LG? +
Yes — Smart IPTV (siptv.app) is available on both Samsung Tizen and LG webOS via their respective app stores. The same NorwayIPTV M3U URL works on both.
What IPTV apps are available on Samsung that aren't on LG? +
SET IPTV and Flex IPTV are Samsung-specific. NET IPTV and Nanomid are more common on LG. Smart IPTV works on both.
Is the 4K IPTV quality the same on Samsung and LG? +
Yes — 4K stream quality from NorwayIPTV is the same on both. The display panel quality (QLED vs OLED) affects picture quality more than the TV's IPTV app.
Can I use IPTV Smarters Pro on Samsung or LG TV? +
Not directly — IPTV Smarters Pro is not in Samsung Smart Hub or LG Content Store. Use Smart IPTV, SET IPTV (Samsung) or NET IPTV (LG) instead. These apps support the same M3U URL.
Which TV brand has more IPTV app choices? +
Samsung has slightly more IPTV app options in Smart Hub (Smart IPTV, SET IPTV, Flex IPTV). LG has Smart IPTV, NET IPTV and Nanomid. Both are well-served.

Samsung Tizen vs LG webOS for IPTV — Tekniske Forskjeller

Samsung Tizen og LG webOS er de to dominerende Smart TV-plattformene i Norge. For IPTV-bruk er det viktige tekniske forskjeller mellom dem som påvirker hvilke apper og metoder som er tilgjengelige.

Samsung Tizen har Smart IPTV som den mest brukte IPTV-appen — den støtter M3U-lister og EPG direkte på TV-en uten ekstern enhet. Alternativt kan TiviMate sideloades via en nedlastet APK, men dette er noe mer teknisk. Den enkleste løsningen på Samsung er Smart IPTV (betales med en éngangsavgift på ca. €5.99) kombinert med NorwayIPTV sin M3U URL. LG webOS har et liknende alternativ kalt Nanomid IPTV, som fungerer på samme måte og er lett tilgjengelig fra LG Content Store.

For de som ønsker det beste IPTV-oppsettet på enten Samsung eller LG — med full EPG, catch-up, og DVR — anbefales det å koble til en ekstern Android TV-enhet (NVIDIA Shield, Mi Box, eller Firestick 4K) til HDMI-porten. Dette omgår begrensningene i TV-ens innebygde app-plattform og gir tilgang til TiviMate og Smarters Pro med alle funksjoner. Firestick 4K Max er den rimeligste og mest kompakte løsningen til under 600 NOK.
