Cloud Streaming vs IPTV — What the Difference Means for Norway

Cloud streaming services — Netflix, Viaplay, Disney+ — store content in data centres and deliver it on demand when you press play. IPTV delivers live channels in real-time, exactly as they are broadcast, without any on-demand library management on the viewer's side. The two models solve different problems, and most Norwegian households benefit from using both.

Where cloud streaming leads: on-demand libraries with exclusive original content (Netflix Originals, Viaplay Originals), better mobile app interfaces, and offline download support for watching without internet. Where IPTV leads: live TV — sport, news, and scheduled broadcasts — without paying for individual streaming service subscriptions; and international channels that no cloud streaming service carries.

NorwayIPTV as a cloud IPTV service delivers its streams from servers in Northern Europe via CDN infrastructure, which means the delivery mechanism is technically similar to cloud streaming — over the internet, to any device, anywhere. The difference is the content type: live linear television rather than on-demand catalogues. For live sport in Norway, where rights are spread across Viaplay, TV2, and MAX, NorwayIPTV's IPTV approach gives access to all three in one place.
