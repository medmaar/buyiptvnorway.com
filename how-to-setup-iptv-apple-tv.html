Apple TV IPTV Setup — 6 Steps

1

Open the App Store on Apple TV

From Apple TV home screen, open the App Store. You need an Apple ID signed in.

2

Search for an IPTV app

Search 'IPTV Smarters Pro' or 'GSE Smart IPTV' or 'iPlayTV'. All are available on tvOS App Store. Install your preferred player.

3

Open IPTV Smarters Pro

Launch IPTV Smarters Pro on Apple TV → Add User → Xtream Codes API → enter your NorwayIPTV portal URL, username and password.

4

Or use GSE Smart IPTV

Launch GSE Smart IPTV → M3U Remote URL → paste your NorwayIPTV M3U URL. GSE Smart IPTV has excellent EPG support on Apple TV.

5

Wait for channels to load

All 50,000+ NorwayIPTV channels appear in 30–60 seconds. Navigate with the Apple TV Siri Remote.

6

Use AirPlay as backup

If you prefer watching on Apple TV without a native app, AirPlay from your iPhone or iPad — stream NorwayIPTV from IPTV Smarters Pro on your phone to Apple TV.

IPTV on Apple TV — Full Guide 2026

Apple TV runs tvOS — Apple's TV operating system. Unlike Firestick or Android boxes, Apple TV does not support sideloading. All apps must come from the tvOS App Store. This means your IPTV player must be officially available on tvOS. Fortunately, IPTV Smarters Pro, GSE Smart IPTV and iPlayTV are all available on tvOS.

IPTV Smarters Pro on Apple TV

IPTV Smarters Pro on tvOS is the same app as the iOS version, adapted for the Apple TV remote. Open the App Store on Apple TV, search 'IPTV Smarters Pro', install (free), then enter NorwayIPTV Xtream Codes. The interface uses the Siri Remote for navigation — swipe to browse channels, click to play.

GSE Smart IPTV on Apple TV

GSE Smart IPTV (tvOS) is highly regarded for its EPG quality. Go to App Store → search 'GSE Smart IPTV' → install → Remote URL → paste NorwayIPTV M3U URL. The EPG guide is particularly good for Norwegian channels.

Apple TV vs Firestick for IPTV

Apple TV is better for: 4K Dolby Vision picture quality, Siri integration, AirPlay ecosystem, long-term software support. Firestick is better for: lower cost (€30–50 vs €150–200), more IPTV app options (sideloading), simpler setup. Both work perfectly with NorwayIPTV. See our full Apple TV vs Firestick comparison.

Apple TV IPTV — FAQ

What is the best IPTV app for Apple TV? +
IPTV Smarters Pro, GSE Smart IPTV and iPlayTV are the best IPTV apps for Apple TV (tvOS). All support NorwayIPTV M3U and Xtream Codes.
Does IPTV work on Apple TV 4K? +
Yes — NorwayIPTV works on Apple TV 4K (2nd and 3rd generation) via IPTV Smarters Pro or GSE Smart IPTV. Native 4K HDR playback is supported.
Can I use AirPlay for IPTV on Apple TV? +
Yes — stream from IPTV Smarters Pro on your iPhone or iPad to Apple TV via AirPlay. This is the simplest method if you don't want to install a separate tvOS app.
Is there a free IPTV app for Apple TV? +
GSE Smart IPTV has a free tier. IPTV Smarters Pro is free to download. The content subscription (NorwayIPTV from €9/month) is separate from the app.
Does IPTV work on Apple TV 3rd generation? +
Apple TV 3rd generation does not have an App Store. Use AirPlay from an iPhone or iPad running IPTV Smarters Pro instead.
Can I watch IPTV Premier League on Apple TV? +
Yes — NorwayIPTV includes IPTV Premier League, Champions League and all sports on Apple TV 4K in full HD and 4K.
