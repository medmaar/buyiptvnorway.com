TiviMate vs IPTV Smarters Pro — Expert Review 2026

Both TiviMate and IPTV Smarters Pro are excellent IPTV players that work perfectly with NorwayIPTV. The right choice depends on which devices you use and which features matter most.

TiviMate — Why It's the #1 IPTV Player on Android/Firestick

TiviMate was built specifically for the Android TV and Firestick experience — large buttons, remote-friendly navigation, and the best EPG in any IPTV app. The free version is generous. TiviMate Premium (€3.99/year via TiviMate Companion) adds recording, multi-screen PiP and up to 5 simultaneous playlists. On Firestick, TiviMate is especially strong because it's in the Amazon App Store — no sideloading required.

IPTV Smarters Pro — Why It Wins on Multi-Platform

IPTV Smarters Pro runs on Android, iOS, tvOS (Apple TV), Windows and Mac — no other IPTV player covers all platforms. If your household uses a mix of Firestick, iPhone, PC and Apple TV, Smarters Pro gives everyone the same app and same NorwayIPTV credentials. The app is 100% free with no feature restrictions — recording is the only gap versus TiviMate Premium.

Which Player Has Less Buffering?

Buffering is determined by the IPTV provider (server quality) and your internet connection — not the player. Both TiviMate and Smarters Pro have essentially identical buffering performance on NorwayIPTV. The choice of player will not affect your stream quality.

TiviMate vs Smarters Pro — FAQ

Is TiviMate or IPTV Smarters Pro better? +
For EPG and live TV experience, TiviMate wins. For multi-platform availability (including iOS and Mac), IPTV Smarters Pro wins. Both work perfectly with NorwayIPTV.
Is TiviMate free? +
TiviMate IPTV Player is free. TiviMate Premium (via TiviMate Companion App) costs ~€3.99/year or ~€0.99/month and adds recording, multi-screen and multiple playlists.
Does IPTV Smarters Pro work on iPhone? +
Yes — IPTV Smarters Pro is available on iOS via the App Store. TiviMate is Android/Firestick only (no iOS version).
Which has better EPG — TiviMate or Smarters Pro? +
TiviMate's EPG is generally rated as better — cleaner layout, faster loading, and more intuitive scheduling. Smarters Pro EPG is functional but less polished.
Can I use both TiviMate and Smarters Pro? +
Yes — install both on your Android device or Firestick. Use TiviMate as your daily IPTV player and Smarters Pro as backup. Both use the same NorwayIPTV credentials.
Which IPTV player should I choose for Firestick? +
TiviMate is the preferred choice for Firestick — it's in the Amazon App Store (no sideloading) and has the cleanest TV interface. IPTV Smarters Pro on Firestick requires sideloading.
