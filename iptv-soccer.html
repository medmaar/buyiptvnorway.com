What is IPTV Soccer?

IPTV soccer means streaming live football matches through an IPTV service using your internet connection, rather than a cable or satellite subscription. With NorwayIPTV, you access all soccer broadcasts through a simple M3U link or Xtream Codes on any device.

IPTV Soccer vs Cable in Norway

To get Premier League, Champions League and Eliteserien on cable/satellite in Norway, you would need Viaplay (NOK 299/mo), TV2 Play (NOK 199/mo) and a sports package — adding up to NOK 500–800/month. NorwayIPTV includes all of this and more from €9/month.
