6-Step Guide

Install IPTV on Smart TV — Step by Step

1

Open your Smart TV App Store

Press Home on your remote → navigate to the App Store (Samsung Smart Hub, LG Content Store, Sony Google Play, etc.).

2

Search for an IPTV app

Search 'Smart IPTV' (Samsung/LG), 'SET IPTV' (Samsung/LG), or 'IPTV Smarters Pro' (Android TV/Sony). Install the app.

3

Find your TV MAC address

Open the installed IPTV app on your TV. The MAC address displays on the welcome screen — note it exactly.

4

Load your M3U URL via web panel

On your phone or PC, visit siptv.app (Smart IPTV) or setiptv.com (SET IPTV). Enter your MAC address, paste your NorwayIPTV M3U URL, click Upload.

5

Activate (Smart IPTV only)

Smart IPTV requires a one-time licence (~€5.49). Pay once, use forever. SET IPTV is free.

6

Relaunch the app on your TV

Go back to your Smart TV, open Smart IPTV or SET IPTV. All 50,000+ NorwayIPTV channels load automatically with EPG.

💡 Pro Tip

For Samsung Tizen TVs older than 2016, sideloading is required. For Sony Android TV, IPTV Smarters Pro installs directly from Google Play — no web panel needed.

How to Install IPTV on Smart TV — Full Guide

Installing IPTV on Smart TV varies by operating system. Samsung uses Tizen, LG uses webOS, Sony uses Android TV or Google TV, Hisense uses Vidaa, TCL uses Android TV or Roku. Each platform has its own recommended IPTV app, but all use the same NorwayIPTV M3U URL or Xtream Codes credentials.

IPTV on Samsung Smart TV — Best Method

Samsung Tizen TVs use Smart IPTV (siptv.app) or SET IPTV (setiptv.com). Both are in Samsung Smart Hub. The setup flow: install app → note MAC address → visit web panel on phone/PC → enter MAC + NorwayIPTV M3U URL → upload → relaunch on TV. Channels load in under 30 seconds.

IPTV on LG Smart TV — Best Method

LG webOS supports Smart IPTV, NET IPTV and Nanomid from the LG Content Store. Setup is identical to Samsung — web panel loading. LG also supports direct Xtream Codes input in some app variants.

How to Fix IPTV on Smart TV

If channels don't load: (1) verify M3U URL in the web panel is exact — no extra spaces, (2) confirm your NorwayIPTV subscription is active, (3) check internet speed (minimum 10 Mbps for HD, 25 Mbps for 4K), (4) restart the TV and app. Still stuck? WhatsApp NorwayIPTV support — average response 5 minutes.
