TCL Smart TV models run either Google TV or Roku TV — two different operating systems with different IPTV app support. NorwayIPTV works on both platforms.

IPTV on TCL Google TV

TCL's Google TV models give full access to the Google Play Store, making IPTV Smarters Pro, Smart IPTV and OTT Navigator all directly installable. This is the best TCL platform for IPTV TCL — same apps as Android TV, same experience.

IPTV on TCL Roku TV

TCL Roku TV models run Roku OS, which has a very limited IPTV app selection. The main option is using an HDMI streaming stick (Firestick 4K or Chromecast) plugged into your TCL Roku TV's HDMI port and installing IPTV Smarters Pro on that device — effectively bypassing Roku's limitations.

Older TCL models may use a proprietary OS or a custom Android fork. For these, Smart IPTV via the browser activation method (register MAC address at siptv.eu) often works.
