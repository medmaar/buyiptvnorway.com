Our approach to refunds and service issues

Our Commitment to Satisfaction

NorwayIPTV is committed to delivering a premium IPTV Norway service that meets every expectation. We stand behind our service — which is why we offer a free 24-hour trial before any purchase. Why We Offer a Free Trial

IPTV services depend on many variables outside our control — device compatibility, internet connection quality, browser or app versions, and local network configuration. Rather than offering refunds after the fact, we give customers the opportunity to test everything thoroughly in advance. Our free 24H trial provides full access to all 50,000+ channels, IPTV Premier League, NRK, TV2 and 4K quality — with no credit card required.

Refund Policy

Due to the immediate digital delivery of IPTV credentials upon subscription activation, NorwayIPTV does not offer refunds once credentials have been delivered and the service has been accessed. This is consistent with standard practice for digital streaming and software services under EU Digital Services Regulations (Article 14, Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU).

Customers who subscribe without using the free trial are advised to contact our support team immediately if they encounter any issues. We resolve the vast majority of technical issues within minutes via WhatsApp.

Service Issue Resolution

If you experience any problem with your NorwayIPTV subscription — buffering, channel quality issues, app configuration problems or credential issues — please contact our 24/7 WhatsApp support immediately. Our technical team resolves the majority of issues within minutes. For issues we cannot resolve within 48 hours, we will:

  • Provide a full explanation of the cause
  • Offer a subscription extension proportional to the downtime experienced
  • Or, in exceptional circumstances where service cannot be restored, review refund requests on a case-by-case basis

Exceptional Circumstances

NorwayIPTV may consider refund requests in exceptional circumstances where:

  • The service was never accessible from the date of purchase despite our support team's best efforts
  • A technical failure on our side caused extended service interruption (beyond 48 hours)
  • The subscription was purchased in demonstrable error (e.g. duplicate order)

To raise an exceptional refund request, contact support@buyiptvnorway.com within 7 days of the issue occurring, with full details of the problem experienced.

Chargebacks

We request that customers contact us directly before initiating a chargeback with their payment provider. We work cooperatively to resolve all issues and are committed to fair outcomes. Chargebacks initiated without prior contact may result in account suspension.
