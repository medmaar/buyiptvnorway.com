What is Hisense Vidaa?

4.8/5  ★ 847 reviews 10,000+ customers 99.5% uptime From €9/mo

Hisense Vidaa (Video Display As Application) is Hisense's proprietary smart TV operating system, used on Hisense ULED, QLED and standard 4K smart TVs. Vidaa OS has its own app store with a growing selection of IPTV apps.

IPTV Apps Available on Vidaa

The main IPTV Vidaa app available on the Hisense Vidaa app store is Smart IPTV — the same app that's popular on Samsung and LG TVs. Smart IPTV on Vidaa supports M3U playlists and Xtream Codes, fully compatible with NorwayIPTV.

OTT Navigator for Vidaa

OTT Navigator is also available for Vidaa OS on some Hisense TV models. It offers a more feature-rich IPTV experience with EPG, VOD and Series support.

Sideloading IPTV on Vidaa

Vidaa OS supports sideloading APK files on some models — allowing you to install IPTV Smarters Pro or other Android IPTV apps not available in the Vidaa store.

NorwayIPTV on Hisense Vidaa

Setting up NorwayIPTV on your Hisense Vidaa TV is simple using Smart IPTV:

1. Install Smart IPTV from the Vidaa app store
2. Note your TV's MAC address shown in Smart IPTV
3. Go to siptv.eu on your phone/PC
4. Enter MAC address → paste your NorwayIPTV M3U URL
5. Reload Smart IPTV on your Hisense TV
6. All 50,000+ channels appear instantly

Alternative: IPTV Smarters APK on Vidaa

If your Hisense Vidaa model supports APK sideloading: download the IPTV Smarters Pro APK on a USB drive → plug into your Hisense TV → install via Vidaa file manager → add NorwayIPTV Xtream Codes credentials.

Hisense Vidaa IPTV — Complete Guide 2026

Vidaa vs Android TV for IPTV

Hisense Vidaa OS has a more limited app store than Android TV, which can restrict IPTV app choices. The key advantage of Vidaa is its tight integration with the TV hardware — Smart IPTV on Vidaa tends to be more stable than sideloaded APKs. If maximum IPTV app choice matters, consider a Hisense TV that includes Google TV (some Hisense models now ship with Google TV instead of Vidaa), which gives access to the full Google Play Store.

Hisense Vidaa Models with Best IPTV Support

For best IPTV Vidaa experience, look for Hisense models with: Vidaa U6 or later (for latest app store), HDMI 2.1 for 4K/120Hz (if using external IPTV box), and USB port for APK sideloading. The Hisense U8N, U7N and E7N series (2024–2026) offer excellent IPTV performance via Smart IPTV on Vidaa OS.

Troubleshooting IPTV on Hisense Vidaa

Common Vidaa IPTV issues and solutions: Smart IPTV not loading channels → re-register your M3U URL at siptv.eu with your TV's current MAC address. Buffering on Hisense Vidaa → connect via Ethernet instead of WiFi if possible. Smart IPTV not available in Vidaa store → try OTT Navigator, or contact NorwayIPTV WhatsApp support for APK sideloading guidance specific to your model.
