Setup Steps

How to Set Up IPTV on Samsung Tizen TV

1

Install Smart IPTV or SET IPTV

Press Home on your Samsung remote → Smart Hub → Search → type 'Smart IPTV' or 'SET IPTV' → Install.

2

Find Your TV MAC Address

Open the installed IPTV app. Your Samsung TV MAC address displays on screen — note it down.

3

Load NorwayIPTV M3U URL

Visit siptv.app (or setiptv.com) on your phone or PC. Enter your Samsung MAC address and paste your NorwayIPTV M3U URL. Click Upload.

4

Launch App on TV

Go back to your Samsung TV, relaunch Smart IPTV or SET IPTV. All NorwayIPTV channels load automatically.

Smart IPTV, SET IPTV, Flex IPTV and NET IPTV are the best IPTV apps for Samsung Tizen. All support M3U playlists and work with NorwayIPTV.
How do I install IPTV on Samsung Smart TV (Tizen)? +
Open Samsung Smart Hub → search for 'Smart IPTV' or 'SET IPTV'. Install the app, visit its web dashboard, enter your Samsung TV MAC address and paste your NorwayIPTV M3U URL.
Does IPTV Smarters Pro work on Samsung Tizen TV? +
IPTV Smarters Pro is not officially in the Samsung Tizen store. Use Smart IPTV, SET IPTV or Flex IPTV instead — all work identically with your NorwayIPTV M3U URL.
What Samsung TV models support IPTV? +
All Samsung Smart TVs with Tizen OS (2016 and newer) support IPTV via Smart IPTV, SET IPTV or similar apps. Older Samsung models need an external IPTV box.
