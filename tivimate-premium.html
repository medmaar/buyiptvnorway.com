Free vs Premium

The free version of TiviMate plays one playlist with the TV-guide interface. TiviMate Premium unlocks the features most viewers end up wanting:

  • Multiple playlists
  • Recording (to local or network storage)
  • Catch-up support where the provider offers it
  • Multi-view (several channels on screen)
  • Favourites management, custom groups and parental controls
  • More layout and EPG customisation

What does it cost?

Premium is sold in the TiviMate Companion app as a yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime licence. Prices vary by country and change over time, so check the Companion app for the current price before buying. One Premium account can be used on several devices, up to the limit the developer sets.

Avoid “free Premium” APKs. Modified versions are a common source of malware and can stop working at any time. Buy Premium only through the official Companion app.

How to Buy and Activate TiviMate Premium

  1. 1
    Install the Companion app — On an Android phone, install “TiviMate Companion” from Google Play.
  2. 2
    Create an account — Sign up in the Companion app and choose the yearly or lifetime option.
  3. 3
    Open TiviMate on the TV — On Firestick or Android TV go to Settings → Premium → Account.
  4. 4
    Sign in — Enter the same Companion account details. Premium activates on that device.
  5. 5
    Add NorwayIPTV — Add Playlist → Xtream Codes → your server URL, username and password.

No Android phone? The Companion app is Android-only; ask a friend's phone to make the purchase, then sign in on your TV with the account.

TiviMate Premium — Common Questions

Is TiviMate Premium worth it?

If you only watch a handful of live channels, the free version is fine. If you want to record, use catch-up, keep several playlists or watch two matches at once, Premium is the upgrade that makes TiviMate stand out.

TiviMate Premium on Firestick

Activation works the same on Fire TV as on Android TV: buy in the Companion app on a phone, then sign in under Settings → Premium on the Firestick.

Recording needs storage

Fire TV sticks have little free space. For recording, add a USB drive via an OTG adapter or point TiviMate to network storage.

Does Premium make streams better?

No. Picture quality and stability come from your IPTV provider, your device and your connection — Premium adds features, not bandwidth.

Do you need TiviMate Premium? A quick decision guide

The free version of TiviMate is enough to watch live channels with a TV guide. Premium is worth it when you want TiviMate to behave more like a full set-top box.

  • Stay on free if you use one playlist, mostly flick between a few channels and don't record.
  • Go Premium if you want favourites and channel groups you can rearrange, catch-up on channels that support it, recording to USB or network storage, reminders, or more than one playlist.

Buying tips

Premium is bought through the official TiviMate Companion app on an Android phone, not inside the TV app, and one account can activate several devices. Prices are set by the developer and change from time to time, so check the current price in the Companion app before you buy, and avoid "lifetime premium" offers from third-party sites, which are often unofficial.

Premium and your IPTV subscription are separate

TiviMate Premium pays for the player. Channels come from your IPTV subscription. Premium doesn't make streams faster or add channels, but recording and catch-up only work if your provider supports them.
