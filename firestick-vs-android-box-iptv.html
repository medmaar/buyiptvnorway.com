Firestick vs Android Box for IPTV — Detailed Analysis

Both devices run Android-based operating systems and support IPTV Smarters Pro, TiviMate and NorwayIPTV. The key differences are in price, power, app ecosystem and connectivity.

Firestick for IPTV — Strengths and Limitations

The Amazon Firestick 4K Max is the most popular IPTV device in Norway for good reason: it costs €50–60, arrives in two days from Amazon.no, plugs into any HDMI port, and runs TiviMate (Amazon App Store) or IPTV Smarters Pro (sideloaded APK) within 15 minutes. The main limitation is the 2 GB RAM — during heavy multitasking or very high-bitrate 4K streams, performance can degrade. The Amazon App Store also lacks some apps available on Google Play.

Android Box for IPTV — Why It Wins on Performance

The NVIDIA Shield TV Pro has 16 GB RAM, a Tegra X1+ processor, built-in Gigabit Ethernet, and runs full Android TV with Google Play Store. IPTV Smarters Pro, TiviMate, Kodi and any VPN app install directly from Google Play — no sideloading. 4K HDR streams run without dropped frames even on high-bitrate channels. The Shield also supports Dolby Atmos for cinema-quality audio.

Budget Android Boxes vs Firestick

Mid-range Android boxes (Xiaomi Mi Box S ~€40, Beelink GT1 Mini ~€60) narrow the price gap with Firestick significantly. They run Android TV, have Google Play, and most have built-in Ethernet or USB-to-Ethernet adapters. If you're between a Firestick and a budget Android box at similar prices, the Android box offers more flexibility for IPTV power users.

Firestick vs Android Box — FAQ

Is Firestick or Android box better for IPTV? +
It depends on your needs. Firestick is more affordable and simpler to set up. Android boxes (especially NVIDIA Shield) offer more power, better 4K performance and more app flexibility.
Can I use the same NorwayIPTV subscription on both? +
Yes — NorwayIPTV works on both Firestick and Android TV boxes. Your subscription includes multi-connection plans, so you can run both simultaneously.
What is the main advantage of Android box over Firestick? +
More processing power, more storage, Ethernet port built-in, Google Play Store access, and compatibility with more apps including full Kodi, TiviMate and IPTV Smarters Pro with no sideloading needed.
Does Firestick support 4K IPTV? +
Yes — Fire TV Stick 4K and 4K Max both support 4K HDR. For the smoothest 4K IPTV, the 4K Max (Wi-Fi 6, 2 GB RAM) is the best Firestick option.
Which is cheaper — Firestick or Android box? +
Firestick starts at €30–50. Budget Android boxes like Xiaomi Mi Box S are €40–60. NVIDIA Shield TV Pro is €220. Firestick wins on price; Android box wins on performance.
Can I use TiviMate on Firestick? +
Yes — TiviMate is available in the Amazon App Store for Firestick. No sideloading needed.
