What is NET IPTV?

NET IPTV is a Samsung Smart TV application available in the Samsung App Store. It supports M3U playlists and Xtream Codes, displaying channels with a clean EPG guide. NorwayIPTV is fully compatible with NET IPTV.

NET IPTV vs SET IPTV vs Smart IPTV

All three are Samsung IPTV apps. NET IPTV requires a small one-time purchase and has a fast, modern interface. SET IPTV is free. Smart IPTV works on LG TVs too. All are compatible with NorwayIPTV.
