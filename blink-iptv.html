What is Blink IPTV Player?

Blink IPTV Player (also called BlinkPlayer) is an IPTV player application supporting M3U playlists and Xtream Codes. It is designed for smooth IPTV playback with a clean, minimal interface. NorwayIPTV is compatible with Blink IPTV Player via M3U URL or Xtream Codes login.

Blink IPTV vs IPTV Smarters Pro

Blink IPTV and IPTV Smarters Pro both support M3U and Xtream Codes. IPTV Smarters Pro has a larger user base and more features; Blink IPTV is lighter and faster for users who prefer a simpler interface. Both work with NorwayIPTV.
