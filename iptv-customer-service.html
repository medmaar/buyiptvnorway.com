App Developer or IPTV Provider?

Players don't sell channels. Apps like IPTV Smarters, TiviMate or SET IPTV are just players. Their developers don't provide streams and can't fix a channel that's down. “IPTV Smarters customer service” can help with the app itself — crashes, activation, purchases — but not with your subscription.

Your IPTV provider handles everything about the service: logins, expired subscriptions, missing channels, EPG problems and buffering on the provider's side.

Quick rule

If the same login works in another app (for example VLC), the problem is the app — contact the app developer. If it fails everywhere, contact your provider.

Getting Good IPTV Support

Check support before you pay

Send a question before subscribing to any IPTV service and see how quickly and clearly it's answered. That's a better guide than star ratings on review sites, which can be hard to verify.

About review sites

When reading IPTV reviews on Trustpilot or Reddit, look for detailed recent reviews describing real setups and support conversations, and be wary of bursts of short five-star reviews.

Protect your account

Real support never needs your password or payment card number over chat. Ignore messages from “support” you didn't contact.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who do I contact if IPTV Smarters isn't working? +

If your login works in another app such as VLC, contact the app developer. If it fails in every app, contact your IPTV provider.

Does IPTV Smarters have customer service for channels? +

No. IPTV Smarters is only a player and doesn't provide channels. Channel, login and subscription issues go to your IPTV provider.

How do I contact NorwayIPTV support? +

Use the WhatsApp button on the website or email contact@buyiptvnorway.com. Include your username (never your password), device, app and a description of the problem.

What information should I send to IPTV support? +

Your username, the device and app, the channel or title affected, the exact error message or a photo, and what you've already tried.

Should I share my IPTV password with support? +

No. Your username is enough to identify your account.

Can I ask questions before buying? +

Yes. Contact support with any question, and use the free 24-hour trial to test on your devices.
