Smarters on Apple Devices: What's Different

On Android the app is called IPTV Smarters Pro. In Apple's App Store the Smarters app is listed as Smarters Player Lite. It plays the same M3U and Xtream Codes logins and is available for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV (tvOS) where the App Store lists it for your region.

Apple TV 4K vs older Apple TV

Smarters and most modern IPTV apps need tvOS on Apple TV HD (4th gen) or Apple TV 4K. The Apple TV 3 and older can't install App Store apps, so “apple tv 3 iptv” setups require AirPlay from an iPhone instead.

Why many Apple TV owners pick a different player

Smarters Player Lite is free and familiar, but Apple TV has some excellent paid players. iPlayTV has a native tvOS interface that suits the Siri Remote, and GSE Smart IPTV handles large playlists well. All of them accept your NorwayIPTV login.

Install Smarters Player Lite on Apple TV

  1. 1
    Open the App Store — On Apple TV, iPhone or iPad, search for “Smarters Player Lite” and install it.
  2. 2
    Choose Xtream Codes — Open the app, accept the terms, then pick Login with Xtream Codes API.
  3. 3
    Enter your details — Any name, then your NorwayIPTV username, password and server URL. The iPhone Remote app makes typing on Apple TV much faster.
  4. 4
    Load content — Live TV, movies, series and EPG download on first launch.
  5. 5
    Tune playback — In Settings → Player, try the alternative player engine if a channel stutters.

Smarters on iPhone, iPad and Apple TV — More Tips

iPhone and iPad

The same Smarters Player Lite app runs on iPhone and iPad. Use it on mobile data with care: HD streams typically use several gigabytes per hour. You can AirPlay a stream from the iPhone to an Apple TV or AirPlay-compatible TV.

Apple TV 4K settings that help

Set Match Content → Match Frame Rate to on in Apple TV settings for smoother sport, and use Ethernet where possible — the Apple TV 4K Ethernet model is noticeably more stable than Wi-Fi in busy apartment buildings.

Firestick vs Apple TV for IPTV

Apple TV has a fast processor and great picture, but no TiviMate. If you want TiviMate specifically, you need an Android-based device — see Apple TV vs Firestick.
